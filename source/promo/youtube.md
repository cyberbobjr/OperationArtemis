## Description

Survival was only the beginning.

A notebook taken from a military infected. A coded broadcast on 108.0 MHz. Someone called V.
Operation Artemis is a story mod for Project Zomboid Build 42: follow the evidence across Knox County, uncover what the army was really doing in the Exclusion Zone, and find a way out alive.

▶ Get it on the Steam Workshop: [WORKSHOP LINK]
▶ Required mod: Signal Smoke — https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3811010882

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔦 FOLLOW THE SIGNAL
Read the notebook, tune a military radio and listen. The investigation starts inside your own survival run: documents, clues and locations reveal your next objective.

📁 UNRAVEL THE OPERATION
Search a bunker under March Ridge, a clinic in West Point, a radio relay and a secret underground base. Recover the evidence, restore power, and make sense of V's trail.

🚨 GET OUT ALIVE
Taking the dossier is only the start. An alarm can turn a quiet search into a desperate climb out of the laboratory. Then choose your exit: helicopter evacuation, the river and its ferryman, or the checkpoint. Each route has its own conditions and its own ending.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

WHAT'S INSIDE
• An investigation woven into the vanilla world, with readable documents and a journal
• Danger with a purpose: power, alarms, security access and a lab escape
• Three exfiltration routes, a victory screen and voiced epilogues
• Optional sterilization countdown and sandbox settings (discovery, intensity, extraction timing)

INFO
• Project Zomboid Build 42.21
• Singleplayer tested; multiplayer not yet validated
• Story text and voiced endings in English and French
