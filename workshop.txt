description=[h1]Operation Artemis[/h1]
description=
description=[b]Survival was only the beginning.[/b]
description=
description=A notebook taken from a military infected. A coded broadcast. Someone called V. Follow the evidence across Knox County, uncover Operation Artemis, and find a way out of the Exclusion Zone.
description=
description=[h2]FOLLOW THE SIGNAL[/h2]
description=
description=Read the notebook, tune a military radio and listen. The investigation starts inside your survival run: documents, clues and locations gradually reveal your next objective.
description=
description=[h2]UNRAVEL THE OPERATION[/h2]
description=
description=Explore a deserted bunker, a clinic, a radio relay and a secret underground base. Recover evidence, restore power when needed and make sense of V’s trail. Taking the dossier is only the start of your escape.
description=
description=[h2]GET OUT ALIVE[/h2]
description=
description=An alarm can turn a quiet search into a desperate climb out of the laboratory. Then choose your exit: helicopter evacuation, the river and its ferryman, or the checkpoint. Each route has its own conditions and ending.
description=
description=[h2]WHAT CHANGES YOUR RUN[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]An investigation built into the existing world, with readable documents and a journal that keeps your discoveries.
description=[*]Danger with a purpose: power, alarms, security access and a laboratory escape.
description=[*]Several exfiltration routes, a victory screen and epilogues tied to your exit.
description=[*]An optional sterilization countdown, plus sandbox settings for discovery, intensity and extraction timing.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]START YOUR INVESTIGATION[/h2]
description=
description=Enable Operation Artemis and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3811010882]Signal Smoke[/url]. Find the Artemis notebook on military infected and read it. Let the clues guide you; the first step does not require debug commands.
description=
description=[h2]BUILD & LANGUAGES[/h2]
description=
description=Project Zomboid 42.21. Core helicopter, ferryman and checkpoint routes tested in singleplayer. Multiplayer and optional integrations still need their own validation. Story text and voiced endings: French and English.
